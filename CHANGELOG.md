# Changelog

## 0.1.1 (unreleased)

- The camera and microphone indicator is named "Camera and Mic", so its name fits.
- Arrange leaves out items macOS is not showing right now, such as Now Playing while nothing plays. They can't be moved until they appear, and then they can be dragged like any other item.
- Camera and Mic can't be dragged in Arrange. macOS always puts it left of the other items.

## 0.1.0

The first public release.

- Hide menu bar items in two sections. Hidden items show with a click on Barbarian's icon, a shortcut, or by resting the pointer on the empty part of the menu bar. Always Hidden items show with an Option-click.
- Hidden items hide again after a delay you choose, or when you click elsewhere.
- A menu lists every item, including items that do not fit beside the notch, and opens the one you pick. Type an item's name to find it.
- Arrange Menu Bar shows the whole menu bar as one strip. Drag items and dividers to rearrange them, and see which items will not fit.
- macOS's own items, such as Wi-Fi and the clock, show symbols that look like them.
- Optionally, each item shows its own icon. This needs Screen Recording.
- Barbarian opens at login and updates itself.

Tested on macOS 15. macOS 26 is supported but not yet tested.
